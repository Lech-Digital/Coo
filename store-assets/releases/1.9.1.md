# Coo 1.9.1 (code 28) — what's new

Play Console → Test and release → the track → Create new release → Release notes. Paste the whole block: Play
reads each language from its tags. At most 500 characters per language; `node store-assets/_src/check-listing.mjs`

<en-US>
Fixes in Coo 1.9.1
• Signing out removes your families’ logs from this phone, after sending anything not yet sent. Sign in again to get them back
• Switching families shows the children, whose family it is and your role, for nannies and grandparents with several families
• The Today headline keeps its size after switching families
• Joining a family removes your old one by itself, so your baby isn’t listed twice
• Premium shows only in the family it was bought for
</en-US>

• Dołączenie do rodziny samo usuwa Twoją starą, więc dziecko nie pojawia się dwa razy
