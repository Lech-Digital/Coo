# Mochi 1.12.1 (code 33) — what's new

1.12.0 plus the fix for the Log's timeline; the notes repeat 1.12.0 for everyone updating from 1.11.0.

Play Console → Test and release → the track → Create new release → Release notes. Paste the whole block: Play
reads each language from its tags. At most 500 characters per language; `node store-assets/_src/check-listing.mjs`
checks it.

```

A fresh matcha look, and Mochi learns more of your baby’s rhythm
• Next feed: when it’s likely, from her own feeds
• Sleep pattern in Stats: the last days side by side, and when her night usually starts
• At night: when she’s likely back asleep, from her own wakings
• This week on Today: what changed since last week
• One tap starts the next breast side, and timers show minutes clearly
• Fixed: tapping the Log’s timeline could close the app
```
