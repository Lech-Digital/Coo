# Icon options

## Mochi, the bunny (2026-11-05, chosen)

The app was renamed from Coo to Mochi (ADR 0011). The icon is Mochi, a bunny-shaped daifuku (usagi mochi), on
matcha `#3F6B4A`. Chosen after looking at about 40 baby-tracker icons on Google Play: many use a moon and a sleeping
baby on purple or navy, or a round baby face. Deep green and animal mascots are rare.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `u6-usagi-one-ear-happy.svg` | **The app icon** and the 512 px store icon: one ear flopped, happy eyes |
| `u1-usagi-ears-up-awake.svg` | **Mochi awake**, beside "Awake for" on Today and in the widget (`Resources/Images/mochi_awake.svg`) |
| `u3-usagi-ears-down-asleep.svg` | **Mochi asleep**, while a sleep runs (`Resources/Images/mochi_asleep.svg`) |
| `u2`, `u4`, `u5` | Other ear and face variants from the round, kept for later |

All are on the 108 grid. The adaptive foreground (`Resources/AppIcon/appiconfg.svg`) uses the view box
`-18 -20 144 144`, so the bunny stays inside the 66 dp safe circle that launchers keep. In the app, Mochi sits on a
matcha disc: a cream bunny on the cream Washi ground would disappear.

## Daifuku (2026-11-05, set aside), in `daifuku/`

The first Mochi icon, a round daifuku with a baby's face on peach. It was in the round-baby-face group that many
trackers already use.

## Coo (2026-10-02), in `coo/`

The drafts from the first icon round. D2 ("saying coo") was the app icon until 1.11.
