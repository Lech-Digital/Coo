# Ideas

Ideas the owner wants kept but not built yet, with where they came from. When one is picked up it moves to a spec or a
release, and out of this list.

## For 1.13.0 or later

From the design and UX reviews of 2026-11-06 (after the matcha identity, ADR 0012):

- **One action zone by day and night (Today).** The day and night layouts share almost no positions, so everything moves
  when the mode flips. Bring a version of the night's big-button grid to day, in the same place, and move the main
  actions nearer the thumb (the running card's button sits in the top third; the tiles' "+" is a 20 dp graphic that
  hides that the whole tile is tappable).
- **"Woke up at 11:23? Adjust"** right on the card after ending a sleep, for "she was still asleep" found later than the
  undo toast lasts.
- **The wet-diaper note needs a next step.** "Fewer than 5 wet diapers a day this week" is a worry with nowhere to go:
  add one tap to "What's normal at her age / when to call", and check how often it fires (the demo baby trips it).
- **A matcha background wash** instead of the lavender, peach and green one: the design reviewer's first pick, to make
  matcha the colour the app is known by. The owner likes the current gradient; keep it unless he changes his mind.
- Small polish from the design review: "More questions" on the Advisor as a plain link, not a fourth button style; the
  sparkle icon instead of the ✨ emoji in the age guide's title; night quick buttons a touch lighter (about 4 %), as they
  read muddy brown; a hint on the moon/sun switch (what Auto does).
- Settings: "What you log" below Baby or on its own page, so a stray tap can't hide a kind.
- A monochrome layer for Android's themed icons.
- The launch film's 16:9 cut for the Play listing (YouTube).
